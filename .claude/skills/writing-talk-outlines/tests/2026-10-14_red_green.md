# Test record: 2026-10-14, mentor catch-up outline

Task given to two fresh general-purpose agents: write the slide-by-slide outline for a
25-minute mentor catch-up from `2026-10-14_mentor_catchup/brief.md` and its sources.

## Without the skill (baseline)

Competent draft, 4 300 words, and the familiar failures:

- Topic-label titles: "Where the benchmark stood in September", "Grey detector I: what a
  real counter sees", "What I need from you". The titles alone did not tell the argument.
- Five bullets per slide, up to 230 characters each, about 900 characters of on-slide text
  per slide. The September failure repeated.
- A list of source files at the top instead of a per-number appendix; no way to check a
  given number.
- One re-derived claim: it combined numbers into "every simulated pair amplitude is 1.7 to
  1.8x below reality; September's 1.65x is superseded", which no source file says.
- Method detail on slides ("Richardson, p = 1.6 to 1.7") that belongs in the talk track.
- Voice mostly plain; one "leverage" (as a verb), long sentences with parentheses.

## With the skill (writer prompt, no editor pass yet)

- Every heading a full sentence; the ghost deck reads as the argument.
- Three or four bullets per slide, all under 170 characters; talk tracks 62 to 151 words.
- A 90-row Numbers appendix with sources; pending items labelled pending; the one thing no
  source gave ("candidate venues") marked SOURCE MISSING rather than invented.
- Softness words used as specified ("estimate, not measured", "indicative", "no result").

Failures the skill did not prevent, fed back as corrections and then into the rules:

- Headlines too long: 11 of 15 over 120 characters (three lines at 24 pt). Budget added
  to the format and the audit.
- One wrong claim in a backup headline: "both estimators sit 10 to 12 % below point
  kinetics" where the sources say the window-fit alphas are steeper than PK and the
  late-time fundamental agrees within 2 %. The sources were read; the sentence
  compressed two facts into a false one. Rule added: a headline that compares two
  numbers names the quantity and the direction exactly as the source does.
- A prediction stated as a measurement in a headline ("the tally-assembly cost factor
  saturates near 6"); the bullets said "predicted". Rule: the softness word goes in the
  headline too when the headline carries the number.
- A SOURCE MISSING placeholder left inside a slide table. Rule: placeholders stay in the
  appendix; the slide poses the question.
- Appendix sources given as bare file names after the first mention, and tables of twenty
  numbers listed one by one. The audit now accepts a bare name once the full path appeared,
  and the format gained `Table source:`.
- The same number written two ways on one slide (0.41 in a bullet, 41 % in the headline).
  Rule: one spelling per number across the outline.

## Verdict

The skill removed every baseline failure that is about structure, numbers and honesty.
What it did not remove is compression error in headlines, which is a reading task for the
main agent and the user, not a rule. Kept as a standing check in step 5 (read the ghost
deck against the sources).
